Multilingual Machine-Generated Text Detection

A detector that distinguishes human-written from LLM-written student text across English, Hindi, Telugu, and Romanised code-mixed input. It fuses stylometric features with perplexity-curvature, calibrates a false-positive guarantee per language, and abstains when unsure — returning human / abstain / machine as decision support for a reviewer.
